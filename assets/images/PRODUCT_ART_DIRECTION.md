# Aura & Oak visual direction

Do not use smoked amber glass as the primary product direction.

## Vessel system
- Cedar: matte charcoal glass soy candle, walnut lid
- Fig Leaf: pale sage-green ceramic soy candle, matching ceramic lid
- Diffusers: sculptural ceramic oil diffusers in off-white, clay, and dark charcoal; fitted lids
- Candle collections: hand-poured soy candles in charcoal and cream vessels, including lidded jars

## Product branding
Print the brand directly on the vessel, never on a floating cream paper label:
- AURA & OAK
- The quiet ritual of home.

## Photography
Pale warm-grey studio backgrounds, soft top light, light shadows, refined minimal home-fragrance photography. Use matte and ceramic textures; no generic smoky/amber-glass candle imagery.

## Applied vessel typography and lid rules
- Put the scent name above the AURA & OAK mark on the vessel.
- Print or emboss AURA & OAK on the lid.
- Vary gallery composition: some main shots have the lid on the vessel; alternate angles may place the lid upright beside the vessel or lay it on its edge alongside the candle.
